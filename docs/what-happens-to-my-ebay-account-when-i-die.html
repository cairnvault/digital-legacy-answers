What happens to my eBay account when I die?

FULLY SOURCED

eBay runs a dedicated, named process — a "Deceased member report form" — and its own help page is direct about it: "We understand that losing someone close to you can be very difficult. If a friend, family member or associate has passed away and had an active eBay account, you can use our Deceased member report form to get help." Source

Anyone can file the initial report — not just the executor. It asks for "your full name, contact details, relationship to the deceased and the member's details, including their full name, birth date, death date, and username if known," plus "an official copy of the death certificate." That much gets the account flagged and current activity addressed. Two further, heavier actions require more:

  • Releasing a positive account balance requires "a court order admitting the deceased's will into probate and/or a copy of the court order appointing the executor of the deceased's estate," plus the requestor's own photo ID "with all information displayed."
  • Closing the account requires the same court-order documentation, plus photo ID — though here eBay explicitly allows redaction: "You may hide the ID number, title, place of birth, nationality, date of issue, height, eye color, photo, and signature as long as the ID card remains recognisable as a valid form of ID."

eBay flags a real timing risk for anyone mid-transaction: "account closure may take up to 60 days if there have been any transactions, payments, claims or disputes in the previous 30 days" — a seller's estate can't necessarily close things out quickly if there's an open dispute or a recent sale still working through eBay's system.

Notably, the help page we fetched describes only closure and balance release — it says nothing about transferring a shop, its feedback history, or its follower base to another person, and does not mention any process for a family member to keep a deceased seller's business running under the same account.

What we could not verify: whether eBay offers any informal, undocumented path to keep a seller account (and its feedback score) active under new management — the fetched page describes closure and fund release only, and is silent on the question rather than explicitly ruling it out, so treat the absence as "not described here," not as a confirmed "no."
