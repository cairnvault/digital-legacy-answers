What happens to my Etsy shop when I die?

How fresh is the evidence under this answer? Etsy’s deceased-members policy behind this answer was last readable on 15 July 2025, via the Internet Archive — and the archived page carried its own footer date of April 2021, so it was already old when captured. Etsy returns HTTP 403 to us.

Flagged by our own source audit on 2026-08-11. Tell us if this is now out of date and we will correct it with a dated note.

FULLY SOURCED

Etsy's own published policy is short, direct, and — like most of this set — draws a hard line between "we'll close it" and "we'll let you in." Etsy's House Rules state: "If you're an estate executor or next of kin, please know that Etsy takes account privacy very seriously, even after a member has passed away. While we're unable to offer access to a deceased member's account, we can work with you to close the account and, in some cases, provide certain content from the account. To ensure that you're authorized to act on behalf of the deceased, we'll ask you to provide certain documentation, such as a death certificate." Source, retrieved via a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 15 July 2025 — Etsy's live legal pages returned a bot-protection block to every direct fetch attempt in this pass

Even reporting a death doesn't require being the executor: "If you know an Etsy member who has passed away, please reach out to our specially-trained team via our Help Center for assistance. Be sure to include the member's full name and email address." That's the low-bar path anyone — a friend, a customer, a fellow seller — can use just to flag the situation; actually closing the account or getting content out of it is reserved for whoever can document they're authorized to act for the estate.

The practical stakes for an Etsy seller specifically are higher than for a typical social account: a shop can carry an active inventory, pending orders, and a payment-processing relationship (Etsy Payments), none of which the policy page addresses directly — it covers account closure and content release, not what happens to open orders, listed items, or funds already owed to the seller while the closure request is pending. Nothing in the sourced text describes a transfer mechanism that would let an heir keep the shop, its reviews, or its follower base running under new ownership.

What we could not verify: what happens to a shop's pending orders, listed inventory, or an Etsy Payments balance specifically while a closure request is under review — the policy text covers the request and closure mechanics, not the shop's operational state during that window. We also could not independently confirm whether this policy page (last dated April 23, 2021, per its own footer) still matches Etsy's current live practice, since the live page blocked every direct fetch attempt.
